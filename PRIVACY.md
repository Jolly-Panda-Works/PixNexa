# Privacy Policy for Image Format Converter

Image Format Converter is an open-source, local-first utility.

- **No data collection:** it does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, analytics, or telemetry.
- **Local processing:** every image is read and converted on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded.
- **No network access:** the app never connects to the internet. The "Website", "GitHub" and "Releases" buttons
  simply open those pages in your web browser.
- **Settings:** your last-used options (format, quality, output folder, switches) are stored in
  `%APPDATA%\ImageFormatConverter\settings.json` on your computer. Delete the folder to remove them.
